# Privacy Policy for Expense Tracker

**Effective Date:** September 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to Expense Tracker. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you use our mobile application and tell you about your privacy rights.

## 2. Data We Collect
We collect the following types of information:
- **Identity Data:** includes email address (via our authentication provider, Clerk).
- **Financial Data:** includes your self-reported expenses, categories, and amounts (stored securely on Supabase).

## 3. How We Use Your Data
We will only use your personal data for the core functionality of the app:
- To register you as a new user.
- To manage and display your expenses.
- To securely sync your transactions across devices.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:
- [Clerk (Authentication)](https://clerk.dev/privacy)
- [Supabase (Database)](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- [Expo (Analytics & Crash Reporting)](https://expo.dev/privacy)

## 5. Data Security
We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way. 

## 6. Your Rights
Under certain circumstances, you have rights under data protection laws in relation to your personal data, including the right to request access, correction, erasure, or restriction of processing.
